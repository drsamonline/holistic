The app

Built to be read at a glance

Not a dashboard of tables. Each screen answers one question, and the ones you open daily are one tap from the bottom bar.

What's inside

Small on purpose

Every system computes rather than looks up wherever it can — the moon is real astronomy, the name numbers are real arithmetic, and nothing is hard-coded to a year.

#

Numerology that computes

Power number from your birth date, personal year, expression number and soul urge from the actual letters of your name. Personal days are derived, not a fixed list.

☾

Real lunar astronomy

Phase, illumination and the next four phase dates calculated from the synodic month, anchored to the new moon of 6 January 2000.

∿

Thirty days at a glance

Physical, emotional and intellectual cycles drawn as a chart you can switch between 7, 30 and 90 days.

∞

Couple, without the verdict

Two dates, two elements, two numbers — scored, explained, and framed as a conversation rather than a ruling.

▤

Printable report

One page, print-friendly, with a plain-text copy button. No PDF library, no export service.

⤓

Backup you control

Export your profile to a JSON file and import it on another device. Nothing syncs behind your back.

What it is

A reflective exercise. The numbers give you a vocabulary for things you already half-know, and the cycles give you a reason to notice your own energy. That is the whole claim.

What it is not

  • Not a diagnosis or a medical device
  • Not a prescription of any kind
  • Not a prediction of your future
  • Not evidence — biorhythm in particular has failed validation since the 1970s

Built on the shoulders of public work

The lunar maths follows the standard synodic-month model; the letter values follow Pythagorean numerology; the trigram and tarot texts are drawn from the public-domain tradition. Full list of sources and their licences in ATTRIBUTION.md.
